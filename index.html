👋 About Me

Hi, I'm Chenxu Zhang (张宸旭). I am an MSc student in Applied Machine Learning at Imperial College London and an Agentic RL intern at Meituan LongCat. I received my bachelor's degree in Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering from Tongji University.

My research focuses on agentic reinforcement learning, on-policy distillation, and agent harnesses. I am interested in how agents learn from interaction, reuse experience as skills, and improve the workflows around language models. My recent work also studies the reliability, security, and evaluation of these systems.

Previously, I interned at PKU OpenDCAI, working on agent workflows and skills, and at Momenta and Siemens. I also contribute to Hello-Agents, an open-source tutorial on building agents from scratch.

Feel free to reach out by email to discuss research and collaboration.
